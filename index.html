What We Do

One crew. Every phase of your dirt work.

From the first pass of the brush cutter to the final grade, JSB Grading handles your project start to finish — no juggling subcontractors.

  1. 01 Residential Grading Building pads, backfills, leveling, and precise final grades for new construction and re-grades. →
  2. 02 Brush Cutting & Bush Hogging Overgrown acreage tamed fast — our cutter handles trees up to 5 inches in diameter. →
  3. 03 Retaining Walls & Hardscaping Rock retaining walls, stone work, patios, and walkways built to hold their ground. →
  4. 04 Land Clearing Wooded lots, fence rows, shooting lanes, and trail construction — cleared clean and ready to use.
  5. 05 Driveways New gravel driveways plus grading, repair, and stone for drives that have seen better days.
  6. 06 Drainage Solutions Standing water fixed at the source: drainage installation and repair, silt fence, and pond maintenance.
  7. 07 Demolition Old structures, slabs, and debris taken down and hauled off so your site starts clean.
  8. 08 Fill Dirt & Aggregate Quality fill dirt, gravel, and stone delivered and spread exactly where your project needs it.

About JSB Grading

Four decades in the dirt. One name on every job.

JSB Grading LLC is owned and operated by Jeff Bayne, who has spent more than 40 years in the grading business — and his whole life around it. When you hire JSB, the person who quotes your job is the same person running the machine.

Based in Tigerville and serving the Upstate since 2013, Jeff has built his reputation the old-fashioned way: showing up on time, doing the work right, and following up after the job is done.
