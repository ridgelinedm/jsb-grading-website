Residential Grading

Building pads, yard leveling, and final grades that send water away from your home — not into it.

Grading is the foundation under everything else on your property. Get it right and your foundation stays dry, your yard drains after a storm, and your driveway holds its shape for years. Get it wrong and you'll be paying someone to fix it — usually after the damage is done.

Jeff has spent more than 40 years grading residential properties across the Upstate, and he grades for the conditions we actually have here: red clay that sheds water, steep lots, and summer storms that dump inches of rain in an afternoon.

What we grade

  • Building pads — scraped, leveled, and compacted pads ready for your builder, garage, barn, or shop.
  • Yard leveling & re-grades — smooth out rough, lumpy, or washed-out ground into a yard you can actually mow and use.
  • Backfills — around new foundations, walls, and utility trenches, filled and compacted properly.
  • Final grades — the finished surface, sloped to drain and ready for seed or sod.
  • Drainage-smart slopes — positive grade away from the house, with swales where the water needs a path.

Why proper grading matters

Most wet basements, cracked slabs, and washed-out driveways in the Upstate trace back to one thing: ground that slopes the wrong way. Water that should shed away from the house instead sits against the foundation or carves gullies through the yard.

A proper grade fixes that at the source. It's far cheaper to move dirt once than to fight water every spring.

How it works

  • Free site analysis — Jeff walks the property with you and gives you an honest read on what it needs.
  • Clear quote — you'll know the scope and the price before any machine shows up.
  • The work — Jeff runs the equipment himself, start to finish.
  • Follow-up — after the next good rain, we make sure the grade is doing its job.
